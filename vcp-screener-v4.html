📊 What is a VCP?
Volatility Contraction Pattern — popularised by Mark Minervini. Stock moves sideways after a big run, forming progressively tighter contractions.

Step 1: Stock is in a Stage 2 Uptrend — Price above MA50 & MA200, MA50 above MA200.

Step 2: Price corrects, then consolidates. Each correction is shallower and shorter — like a coil tightening.

Step 3: Volume dries up during the base — institutions done selling.

Step 4: A breakout on EXPANDED VOLUME through the pivot confirms the next move up.

The classic 3-contraction VCP: 25% → 12% → 5%. Final contraction ideally under 10%.
📈 The 5 Key Areas
✓ Categories & Catalysts — Market leader, top competitor, institutional favorite, turnaround
✓ Price & Volume — Volume drying up in base, expanding on breakout
✓ Company Fundamentals — Sales, Earnings, Margins, Ratios
✓ Entry Points — Pivot above the base on volume
✓ Exit Points — Stop hit = sell, no debate
🏆 The 95% Club Criteria
The biggest winning stocks share these traits:

99% were above their 200-day MA
96% were above their 50-day MA
95% had a relatively small float
95% showed earnings acceleration
96% began from correction or consolidation periods
70% reported earnings +20% in the most recent quarter

Focus on Consumer, Technology, Pharma/Biotech, Infra sectors.
🏦 Fundamentals to Focus On
✓ Earnings growth — Accelerating QoQ and YoY EPS
✓ Sales increase — Revenue growing last 3–4 quarters
✓ Strong margins — EBITDA margin expanding
✓ Low debt — D/E under 1.0 preferred
✓ Altman Z-Score > 3 — Safe zone, no distress

Top 3 ways for a company to grow earnings:
Sell More · Raise Prices · Cut Costs
📉 Earning Maturity Cycle
Stage 1 — Value stock bottoming, positive surprise coming
Stage 2 — EPS momentum, estimate revised up → GROWTH STOCK ← BUY HERE
Stage 3 — Peak growth, topping out
Stage 4 — Loss of EPS momentum, estimate revised down → VALUE TRAP

The VCP works best in Stage 2. Avoid Stage 3 and 4 entries entirely.
⚠ Worst Candidates to Avoid
✗ Capital intensive — low ROIC, heavy capex
✗ Limited pricing power — commodity businesses
✗ Heavily regulated — policy risk at every turn
✗ Margin pressure — EPS declining despite revenue
✗ Eroding industry position — losing market share
✗ Penny / tips / rumour stocks — no fundamentals

Verify all fundamentals on Screener.in or NSE/BSE before entering.
